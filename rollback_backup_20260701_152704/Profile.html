Song Registration for PRS / CAE / IPI / SESAC / ASCAP / BMI Pro

Each songwriter can have a different collection society. Split share must total 100% across all songwriters.

Why register your song?

Registering your song with PRS for Music helps protect your rights and ensure you receive royalties from performances and streams.

  • Use your CAE/IPI to identify yourself as the composer/author.
  • Include ISRC for the track to track usage across platforms.
  • Registering properly improves rights management and payment accuracy.
  • Now with IPFS: Your song metadata is stored on the decentralized IPFS network. After registration, you will see the IPFS CID (hash) for your song's metadata.
